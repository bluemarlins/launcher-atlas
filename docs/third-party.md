# Third-party files and assets

The MIT license applies to Launcher Atlas code authored in this project. It does not replace upstream licenses of Gradle, AndroidX, Kotlin, Android or installed apps.

Gradle Wrapper files are generated by Gradle 9.1.0. The distribution checksum is pinned in `gradle/wrapper/gradle-wrapper.properties`, and the wrapper JAR checksum is checked against Gradle's published value. See [Gradle](https://github.com/gradle/gradle) for upstream sources and licensing.

Starter configurations bundle no third-party app icon images. The launcher displays installed app icons at runtime. Emulator screenshots show those installed apps; their names and marks belong to their owners.

Added images and fonts require a manifest entry identifying their source and reviewed distribution terms. A manifest entry is a record, not an automatic legal clearance.
